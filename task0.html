Responsive Headings and Paragraphs

This webpage demonstrates responsive typography implemented with CSS media queries. The text sizes dynamically adjust across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.

Viewport Breakpoints

On mobile devices, headings and paragraphs use compact sizes. On tablets, they scale up to medium dimensions, and on desktops, they expand for comfortable reading.
